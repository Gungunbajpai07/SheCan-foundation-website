About She Can Foundation

We are a non-profit organisation focused on creating opportunities for women and girls through education, training, and community leadership.

Our Story

Founded by a group of educators and community leaders, She Can Foundation began with a simple belief: when women are educated and supported, whole communities prosper. Our programs started as local workshops and have grown into sustained initiatives focused on long-term outcomes.

We work closely with local partners, schools, and families to design programs that respect culture and deliver measurable benefits.

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

To empower women and girls to lead healthy, educated, and economically independent lives through programs that are locally-led and sustainably designed.

Vision

A future where every woman and girl can access education, skills and opportunities to shape their own path.

Core Values

  • Respectful partnerships
  • Transparency and integrity
  • Evidence-driven programs
  • Inclusivity and dignity

How We Work

  1. Listen: We engage communities and stakeholders to understand needs.
  2. Design: Co-create culturally-appropriate programs with local partners.
  3. Deliver: Implement with trained staff and volunteers.
  4. Measure: Monitor outcomes and adapt for impact.
